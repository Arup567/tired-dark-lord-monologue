A 100-word villain monologue, entered for the make.fun bounty
[The Tired Dark Lord: A 100-Word Villain Monologue](https://make.fun/bounty/u-kevin-the-tired-dark-lord-a-100-word-villain-58b4f3cb).

A fantasy villain who is secretly very tired, delivering his own scheme in the
half-breath of a man who has stopped caring whether it works. The weariness is
in the rhythm, the pauses and the small deflections, never stated outright:
he calls the crown's herald a nuisance, not a threat, and dismisses his own
grudge against the tax collector. He still has a plan (the cursed bridge, the
map at dusk) and a history (the year the river froze, the grandfather's tax),
and the last line lands on the whole thing.

## Run it

```
node main.ts
```

`main.ts` writes the monologue to `/output/entry.txt`, UTF-8, inside the
gate's 600-character cap (the program asserts the length before writing).
